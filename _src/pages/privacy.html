This is a starting draft and is awaiting legal review under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

SocietalPro (“we”, “us”) is a private company that provides a society management platform for housing societies. This policy covers this website, societalpro.com. The SocietalPro app at app.societalpro.com has its own terms and privacy notice for societies and residents.

What we collect on this website

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone (optional), role, society name, city, number of flats and your message, when you choose to send them.
  • Email and WhatsApp: whatever you choose to share when you write to us.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address and browser type to keep the site secure and working.

Free tools

Our free tools run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us. Some tools save your entries in your own browser (local storage) so you can come back later; you can clear this at any time using your browser settings or the tool’s reset button.

Why we use your data

  • To reply to your question or demo request.
  • To understand which society you represent so we can suggest the right plan.
  • To keep the website secure.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for anything you wouldn’t reasonably expect from contacting us.

Who processes it

Contact form submissions are delivered to our inbox through Web3Forms. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Payments on the SocietalPro app are processed securely via Razorpay; we do not hold your society’s funds.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and then delete them unless you become a customer or ask us to keep in touch.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data you’ve shared with us, or withdraw your consent, by writing to support@societalpro.com. We’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@societalpro.com or message us on WhatsApp.
